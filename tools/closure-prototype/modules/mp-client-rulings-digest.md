CLIENT ISLAND RULINGS DIGEST (DEC-55..DEC-58, full text in docs/decisions.md; survey facts in docs/plans/2026-07-24-client-port/client-engine-survey.md)
- The client island is the second filler of the frozen `EngineHooks` table, and module services choke through the same `EngineHost` seam the server established (DEC-55.1). The dispatchers land in `mp_engine_client` as `cl_cgame.rs` and `cl_ui.rs`. `VmSlot` gains `Uivm` and `Cgvm` becomes live.
- Trap ownership map (DEC-55.2): G2 arms route to `mp_engine_ghoul2`. Renderer arms call the `mp_renderer` frontend directly, and a synchronous trap reads CPU `RenderAssets` only. FX arms route to the section-F FX subsystem - its design ruling is PENDING, so no FX transcription happens from these packets. Sound arms route to `SoundSystem`, the `CG_AS_*` arms to its `snd_ambient` module. CM arms route to the ported `cm_*`. Cvar, cmd, FS, and misc arms route to `mp_engine_qcommon`.
- State law (porting-rules B): no `static mut`, no hidden globals. The survey classifies `clientActive_t`/`clientConnection_t`/`clientStatic_t`/`console_t`/`keyGlobals_t`/`kbutton_t` interiors and all sound state as Class C - engine-private, idiomatic shapes free, threaded through the owning `Client`/`SoundSystem` structs on `Engine`. Embedded Class-B and wire members (entityState_t, playerState_t, usercmd_t, gameState_t, snapshot_t, uiClientState_t, refEntity_t family, glconfig_t) keep their frozen ported types. Class A: `cl.mSharedMemory` is the one engine-retained module pointer, twin of the modeled `sv.mSharedMemory`.
- Platform (DEC-56): the winit pump feeds `sysEvent_t` over a bounded channel into the frozen `SysEventQueue` ring. The `win32/` TUs never port. Gamma, display modes, and the GL loader are superseded by wgpu.
- Sound (DEC-57): the paint pipeline ports faithfully through a `dma_t`-shaped ring at 22050 Hz, and the cpal callback pulls from it. The OpenAL/EAX arm is dropped (rule 20) - EAX-only and AL-only functions carry drop markers, and `s_UseOpenAL` gated branches inside live functions transcribe as dead constant-false arms with a one-line note. `snd_mp3.cpp` wraps minimp3, and the `mp3code/` decoder is replaced, never ported.
- Validation (DEC-58): demo playback is the deterministic input path. The snapshot truncation (`clSnapshot_t` to `snapshot_t`) and the reliable-command copy-out get committed-fixture tests - transcribe them exactly.
- House idiom: qboolean becomes `bool` and out-params become returns at interior seams (porting-rules C7). Latin-1 string discipline per the #13 campaign. Comments in STE form with `Source:` cites.
